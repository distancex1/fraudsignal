AI and payments risk

AI agents are paying on your platform. Who decided which ones to trust?

Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, and OpenAI all have agent payment protocols live. Agents now find, buy, and pay on your customers' behalf, and fraud rings run the same tools.

Most platforms have no policy for it. Controls built for people read a bad agent as a clean transaction. Someone has to decide which agents you accept, how you verify them, and who carries the loss. That is a risk leadership decision.

Synthetic merchants

A document check no longer proves a business is real.

AI now produces the website, formation documents, owner ID, and live selfie. Onboarding has to run on signals that are expensive to fake.
