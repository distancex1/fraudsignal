The Risk Audit outline

Where you're exposed, in dollars, and what to fix first. Here is what I review, what I need from you, and what you get at the end.

Start with an audit See the work
01 · Scope

What gets reviewed

Fraud losses
Your loss history, the controls behind it, and where losses get through.
Merchant onboarding
How merchants are verified and approved, and whether a synthetic business would pass.
Merchant credit
Limits, reserves, and payout timing across the portfolio, and your bust-out exposure.
Disputes
Chargeback and fraud ratios against processor thresholds and Visa VAMP.
Compliance
BSA/AML, KYC, and NACHA programs, tested against what your partners expect.
Partners
Your obligations under processor and sponsor bank agreements.
AI and agents
How your controls treat agent traffic and AI-generated applicants.
02 · Inputs

What I need from you

  • Your processor and sponsor bank, and the agreements with each
  • Payment volume by month
  • Exports of your current fraud rules and alerts
  • Loss and dispute history
  • Any notices or reviews from your processor, bank, or the card networks
  • Your current risk and compliance policies
  • Time with the people who run payments, support, and compliance today
03 · Timeline

How the weeks run

Week 1
Intake. Kickoff, data access, and interviews with your team.
Week 2
Review. I work through the data, test controls, and size each exposure in dollars.
Week 3
Readout. Findings walked through with your leadership team, with time for questions.
04 · Deliverables

What you get

  • Board-ready findings, ranked by dollar exposure
  • A 90-day plan for what to fix first
  • A straight answer on whether you need a full-time hire
