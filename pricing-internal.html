Payment Terms & Policies

  • Payment Schedule: 50% deposit upon contract signing, 50% upon completion (projects) or monthly in advance (retainers)
  • Accepted Methods: ACH, wire transfer, check (no credit cards)
  • Volume Tier Adjustments: If client crosses into higher volume tier during engagement, pricing adjusts at next renewal/project. Current engagement pricing remains locked.
  • Retainer Rollover: Unused hours expire monthly; no rollover or banking
  • Cancellation: 30-day written notice required for retainers; early termination incurs 25% of remaining contract value
  • Expenses: Travel, software licenses, and third-party tools billed at cost (pre-approved)
  • Rate Increases: Pricing locked for 12 months from contract date; 5-10% annual increase typical
Referral Program: Clients who refer a signed engagement receive 10% cash kickback or 1 month free retainer service (their choice).
